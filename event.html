Location & Venue

Naraguta Royal Festival Arena & Hills Amphitheatre
Jos North LGA, Plateau State, Nigeria

•

Day 1: Sacred Rites & Royal Convocation

Opening blessing by His Royal Majesty the Ujah of Anaguta, pouring of libations, and grand parade of the 12 clans.

•

Day 2: The Grand Archery Championship

Warrior bowmen compete in traditional target marksmanship, mountain tracking runs, and martial exhibition.

•

Day 3: Harvest Feast & Musical Jubilation

Showcase of indigenous Anaguta cuisine, horn-blower ensembles, youth cultural dance troupes, and closing honors.
