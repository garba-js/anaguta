Living Endeavors

Documentation · 2026

AFM Cultural Documentation Project

Comprehensive multimedia recording of oral histories, elder genealogies, clan boundaries, and ritual songs across Jos North.

Culture · 2026

Anaguta Love and Culture — Then & Now

An archival photo and interview exhibition examining the evolution of courtship, marriage rites, and community solidarity across generations.

Community · 2026

Youth Heritage & Vocational Empowerment Drive

Hands-on skills workshops in barbering, tailoring, craftsmanship, and entrepreneurship for Anaguta young men and women.

Recreation · 2026

Naraguta Hills Heritage Eco-Hike & Archery Rites

Annual guided mountain expeditions tracing ancestral watchtower caves, natural defensive ridges, and live archery exhibitions.

Music · 2026

Anaguta Traditional Music & Chant Archiving

Digitizing sacred drum rhythms, hunting horns, and choral chants performed during the Ishokori harvest festival.

Outreach · 2026

Plateau Elder Care & Health Screening Clinic

Grassroots health support providing free blood pressure, vision, and wellness screenings for elder community members.
