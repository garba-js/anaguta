Who We Are

A People of
Deep Roots

The Anaguta people possess a unique identity shaped by traditional farming practices, strong family values, a resilient warrior spirit, and rich storytelling traditions passed down through generations in the highlands of Jos North, Plateau State.

Anaguta Tradomodern is committed to sharing this powerful heritage with the world — bridging ancestral wisdom with contemporary expression, educational technology, and community development.
