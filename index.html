Who We Are

A People of
Deep Roots

The Anaguta have a unique identity shaped by their traditional farming practices, sacred archery heritage, warrior spirit, and captivating storytelling passed down through generations in the highlands of Jos North, Plateau State.

Anaguta Tradomodern is committed to sharing this powerful heritage with the world — bridging ancestral wisdom with contemporary expression, educational technology, and community development.
