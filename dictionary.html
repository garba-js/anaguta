Word of the Day
Ishokori
/ee-shoh-KOH-ree/ · noun
The sacred annual cultural harvest thanksgiving festival and warrior archery convocation of the Anaguta people.
"Ishokori ri na biki mai girma a garin Jos."
The Ishokori festival brings our kingdom together in jubilation and unity.
